OS Tapasthali Cottage

A quiet stay in the Mandal Valley, with simple sattvic meals, forest walks and visits to sacred places. A gentle return to a simpler rhythm.

Duration2 nights · 3 daysGroup sizeUp to 10LocationMandal Valley · 2,400 m

A gentle return to simplicity.

Stay in the Mandal Valley and explore the sacred places around it, with private rooms, simple food and space for reflection. The published journey begins and ends in Rishikesh.

Three days in the valley

Day 1 · Rishikesh to Anusuya

Travel into the mountains, with a visit to Dhari Devi temple on the way. Walk to Anusuya temple and stay for evening arti. The itinerary lists a 5 km walk and an overnight stay at Anusuya.

Day 2 · In the footsteps of the sages

Explore Dogra Tapasthali, Atri Cave and the forested paths of Mandal Valley. Offer prayers to Kshetrapal Devta. The itinerary lists a 10 km walk and a night in Mandal Valley, at up to 2,400 metres.

Day 3 · Return to Rishikesh

After breakfast, return to Rishikesh. An optional visit to Kunjapuri Devi temple is described in the published itinerary.

Your stay

  • Private rooms with attached bath and hot water
  • Three simple sattvic meals a day
  • Bedding and blankets
  • Forest walks and a common sitting space kept in silence until midday

Before you arrive

Mobile reception can be patchy. Weather may require an additional night. Please confirm the exact accommodation, transport and guided temple-visit arrangements when booking, and ask in advance if you need mules or extra assistance.

Navaratri Sadhana with Amit Om

An eleven-day Navaratri anushthan in a Himalayan cottage, personally guided from sankalpa to purnahuti. A visit to OS Tapasthali is included.

Duration12 nights · 13 daysGroup sizeUp to 2LocationMandal Valley · 2,400 m

The practice comes first.

An eleven-day residential anushthan through the nine nights of Navaratri, with time for sankalpa and purnahuti. The full stay spans thirteen days, including arrival and return. The vidhi is taught in full, including japa and the daily hom.

The daily rhythm

4:00 am Snan and morning sandhya

5:00 am Yagya

7:30 am Yoga and stretching

8:00 am Breakfast

Afternoon Lunch, yagya kund cleaning sewa, and japa

6:30 pm Evening sandhya and japa

Held with care and discipline

Mauna is observed between the evening sitting and morning sandhya. Devices are put aside during sitting hours, except for sadhana notes or a tablet used for practice. Discuss the dietary discipline with Amit Om before joining.

Your time here

Arrival and sankalpa

Arrive by noon, settle in, and receive orientation. The sankalpa is held in the evening.

The nine nights of practice

Follow the full daily rhythm of sandhya, japa, yoga and yagya. The final night sitting is held longer than the rest.

Purnahuti and OS Tapasthali

The published itinerary places purnahuti and the visit to OS Tapasthali on day eleven, with return towards Rishikesh on day twelve. Confirm arrival and departure arrangements when booking.

What is included

  • Residential stay for the full anushthan
  • Sattvic meals and all puja samagri
  • Hom materials, daily guidance and teaching sittings
  • Purnahuti, prasad and the OS Tapasthali visit

What to arrange separately

  • Travel to the retreat location
  • Your personal asana, mala and clothing
  • Dakshina, at your discretion
